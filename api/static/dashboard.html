Read the evidence, not the number. Per-report scores are a ranker trained on 231 labelled rows, not a verdict. Site risk is an unvalidated aggregation policy — no test set, no PR-AUC — that assumes hazards at a site are independent when they are not, and it inherits and amplifies the model's out-of-domain errors. This triages reports for human review; it does not replace human judgement.
